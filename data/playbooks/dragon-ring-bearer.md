- No Ring Bearer or Persistent Poet seen by turn 6-7 -> stop forcing Dragon Ring Bearer, take the strongest open lane instead.
- Losing combats turn 4-6 despite following the curve -> the pieces aren't coming together fast enough here; pivot.
- Dragon Ring Bearer heavily contested (pieces gone from shops by turn 5) -> pivot early rather than fighting for scraps.
