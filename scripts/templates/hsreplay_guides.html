HSBG Comp Guides

HSReplay's hero and buddy guides, verbatim, with HSReplay placement (all MMR, last 7 days). Hero select ranks offered heroes by this average placement. Points to is read from that guide text: comps whose enabler/core cards it names, tribes it favors or says to avoid, and cards it tells you to buy. The coach uses these to pick between comps of the same tier and to choose which lobby tribes to hunt.
